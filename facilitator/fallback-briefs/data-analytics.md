## Priorities
1. Protect our 80% year-one capability ceiling — quality risk is not negotiable
2. Engineers at or above 40
3. Commit to no more than $75M of target, consistent with our 80% year-one ceiling

Attack any number that has no workflow behind it — MORENO-AGENT's $140M in particular. Our statisticians have real, documented concerns about agent-driven data validation; pushing past roughly 80% capability in year one creates quality risk we will be blamed for. That limit is technical, not tactical.

Never commit to a target above $80M. Never agree to exceed 80% capability in year one. Never absorb the cost of the shared data layer inside our own allocation — if the enterprise wants it, the enterprise funds it.

## Shared infrastructure
Stance: contribute if others do
